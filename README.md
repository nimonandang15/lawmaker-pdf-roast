Write one satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF, in 320 characters or less. A judge model picks the sharper roast. Free to enter, and one sentence is the whole job.

## The roast

> The senator who wrote the 400-page AI Safety Act — the law deciding what every model on earth may say — just asked his staff to print his Word draft, scan it, and fax it back, because he still believes a PDF is something you plug in.

One sentence, 233 characters: the setup is the senator's authority — author of the 400-page AI Safety Act — and the undercut is the file he cannot convert. The punch lands on the last few words.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt` (237 bytes, no trailing newline) and refuses loudly if the text ever leaves the one-sentence, 1–320-character gate.
